Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect information submitted through APNA KAAJ.

Last Updated: 20 October 2026

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

When you use the “Request Service” feature, we may collect the information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Location
  • Selected business/service
  • Required date, if provided
  • Message or additional request details, if provided

The website may also generate a Request ID and submission timestamp for managing your request.

Please do not submit passwords, OTPs, banking PINs, card details, Aadhaar numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information through the Request Service form.

2. WHY WE COLLECT THIS INFORMATION

The information submitted through the website is used only for purposes related to your service request, such as:

  • Understanding which service you require
  • Contacting you regarding your request
  • Scheduling or coordinating the requested service
  • Managing and tracking service requests
  • Providing customer support

3. HOW SERVICE REQUESTS ARE PROCESSED

When you submit a Request Service form through the APNA KAAJ website, the information is sent through our service-request system and may be stored in a Google Sheet used by APNA KAAJ for request management.

A notification containing relevant request details may also be sent to the APNA KAAJ administrative email account so that we can respond to the request.

The Request Service form is displayed directly on the APNA KAAJ website; you are not required to complete a separate public Google Form.

4. GOOGLE SERVICES

APNA KAAJ currently uses Google services, including Google Apps Script, Google Sheets and email services, to receive, store and manage service requests.

Information processed through these services may also be subject to Google's applicable privacy and security practices.

5. WHATSAPP

The website may provide buttons or links that allow you to contact APNA KAAJ through WhatsApp.

If you choose to use WhatsApp, your communication will take place through WhatsApp and will also be subject to WhatsApp's own privacy practices.

Using WhatsApp is optional.

6. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

Information submitted through APNA KAAJ is intended to be used for legitimate business and customer-service purposes related to your request.

We do not intend to sell your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

We do not use the Request Service form to collect payment-card information, banking passwords, OTPs or similar confidential credentials.

7. INFORMATION SHARING

Your service-request information may be accessed by APNA KAAJ personnel who need it to understand, manage or fulfil your request.

Information may also be processed by service providers used to operate the request system, such as Google services.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or a valid legal requirement.

8. DATA RETENTION

Service-request information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Manage and complete your request
  • Maintain relevant business records
  • Resolve customer-service issues
  • Meet applicable legal or administrative requirements

Information that is no longer reasonably required should be deleted or otherwise appropriately handled when practical.

9. DATA SECURITY

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through the website.

However, no internet transmission, email system or online storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

For this reason, please avoid submitting unnecessary confidential or highly sensitive information through the Request Service form.

10. YOUR CHOICES

Providing information through the Request Service form is voluntary.

You may choose to contact APNA KAAJ through other available contact methods instead.

If you have a question about information you previously submitted, or would like to request correction or deletion where appropriate, you can contact us using the details below.

11. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The APNA KAAJ website provides information about local services and is not designed specifically to collect personal information from children.

If a minor needs to submit information for a service request, involvement of a parent or guardian is encouraged where appropriate.

12. EXTERNAL LINKS

The website may contain links to third-party websites or services, including government portals, online services, WhatsApp or other external resources.

APNA KAAJ is not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of third-party websites.

Please review the relevant third party's policies when using those services.

13. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or information-handling practices change.

When significant changes are made, the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page should also be updated.
