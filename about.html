Our Story

One Place for Many Everyday Needs

APNA KAAJ started from a simple idea: people should not have to go to many places for common needs. Today it brings together government and online service help, photography and printing, RO drinking water and a quiet study library.

We focus on our local community in and around Antu, Pratapgarh, and aim to keep every service simple, honest and easy to request.

Four Businesses, One Name

Clean RO water supply for homes, shops and events.
